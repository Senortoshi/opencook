# Build Journal

How OpenCook gets built, in the open: one entry per working session, with what we were trying to do, what we found, what we decided and what's parked.

**Why this exists.** The world taught us scarcity: guard your idea, or someone takes it. OpenCook bets the other way. Post your idea, and everyone who helps cook it gets credit, then gets paid. This journal is the receipts for how we work: research first, honest findings (including the ugly ones), audits before merges, and credit to whoever did the work.

**Where ideas live.** Ideas are claimed **on-chain, as OpenCook posts**, not here. An OC post is signed, timestamped and permanent, and that's the proof of who thought of it first (see DECISIONS.md, "Attribution is timestamp-arbitrated"). This journal records the *process*, not claims.

**Rules for entries**
- Entries here are curated summaries. The full conversations are in [convos/](convos/README.md), written by `scripts/export-convos.mjs`.
- No personal info in entries: names, emails, machine paths, keys, private business details (repo Hard Rule 6). The transcripts in `convos/` are published as typed, with keys, emails and machine paths blanked.
- Say what failed and what's unsure, not just what worked.
- Link to the commit, PR or on-chain post when one exists.

Entries are named `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`, newest at the top of the list below.
